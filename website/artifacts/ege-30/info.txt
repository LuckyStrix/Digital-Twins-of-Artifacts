Name: Ege 30
Type: manuscript
Description: A 15th-century manuscript leaf (Book of Hours) from the Otto
Ege collection in the RIT Cary Graphic Arts Collection. Imaged across 16
wavelengths from UV through near-infrared by MISHA (Multispectral Imaging
System for Historical Artifacts), an independent project of RIT's Cultural
Heritage Imaging, Preservation, and Research program (rit.edu/chipr/misha)
— not one of this project's own capture systems. Preliminary review found
probable undertext visible under UV, indicating this leaf is a palimpsest.
Link: https://www.rit.edu/chipr/misha
