Name: Sumerian Cuneiform tablet
Type: tablet
Description: Clay tablet with text of an administrative nature. The tablet originated from the ancient city of Umma, which is now known as Tell Jokha. It is dated from the period of Ur III (ca. 2100-2000 BCE).  Rights: RIT Libraries makes materials from its collections available for educational and research purposes pursuant to U.S. Copyright Law.
