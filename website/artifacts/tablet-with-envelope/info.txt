Name: Sumerian Cuneiform tablet with envelope
Type: tablet
Description: Clay tablet with text of an administrative nature. The tablet is covered by an unopened clay envelope. The tablet originated from the ancient city of Girsu which is now known as Tello. It is dated from the period of Ur III (ca. 2100-2000 BCE).
